一、故宫是什么 What is the Forbidden City?

神武门与角楼 · 故宫北门 Shenwumen Gate and Corner Tower · North Gate of the Forbidden City

故宫位于北京中轴线的中心，旧称“紫禁城”，是明清两代（1368—1911）的皇宫。曾有24位皇帝在此居住和处理政务。1987年被联合国教科文组织列入世界文化遗产名录。

紫禁城之名源于古代天文学——紫微垣位于天之中，对应天子所居，加之宫禁森严，故称“紫禁”。

The Forbidden City, also known as the Palace Museum, sits at the center of Beijing's central axis. It served as the imperial palace for the Ming and Qing dynasties, housing 24 emperors over nearly 500 years. It was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1987.

The name "Forbidden City" reflects its status as the emperor's residence, where entry was strictly forbidden to commoners.

三、建筑布局：前朝后寝 Architectural Layout: Outer Court & Inner Court

太和殿广场 · 外朝核心 Hall of Supreme Harmony Square · Heart of the Outer Court

故宫建筑分为“外朝”与“内廷”两部分。外朝以太和殿、中和殿、保和殿三大殿为中心，是举行国家大典的地方。内廷以乾清宫、交泰殿、坤宁宫为中心，是皇帝和皇后居住的正宫，两侧为东六宫和西六宫，供妃嫔居住。

这种“前朝后寝”的布局，体现了中国古代宫殿建筑“礼”与“生活”分区的理念。

The Forbidden City is divided into two main sections: the Outer Court and the Inner Court. The Outer Court, centered on the three great halls—Taihe, Zhonghe, and Baohe—was used for grand state ceremonies. The Inner Court, centered on the Qianqing, Jiaotai, and Kunning palaces, served as the residence of the emperor and empress, flanked by the Six Eastern and Six Western Palaces for consorts.

This layout reflects the traditional Chinese architectural principle of separating ceremonial spaces from living quarters.

四、值得记住的建筑细节 Architectural Details Worth Remembering

角楼

Corner Towers

城墙四角各有一座角楼，民间形容其结构为“九梁十八柱七十二条脊”，造型极为精巧，是紫禁城防卫设施的一部分。

The corner towers feature a complex cross-shaped structure with multiple levels of roofs, showcasing extraordinary craftsmanship.

黄色琉璃瓦

Yellow Glazed Tiles

故宫屋顶以黄色琉璃瓦为主，黄色在五行中对应“土”，象征天子居天下之中、统御四方。唯一的例外是藏书楼文渊阁，用黑瓦取“水克火”之意。

Yellow glazed tiles dominate the roofs as yellow symbolized imperial authority. The only exception is the imperial library, which uses black tiles representing water to ward off fire.

太和殿的脊兽

Ridge Beasts of Taihe Hall

太和殿屋顶上有10只脊兽，是中国古建筑中等级最高的配置。

The Hall of Supreme Harmony has ten roof ridge beasts, the highest number found on any ancient Chinese building.

五、从皇宫到博物馆 From Imperial Palace to Public Museum

神武门 · “故宫博物院”匾额 Shenwumen Gate · Plaque of the Palace Museum

1911年辛亥革命推翻帝制，1925年10月10日故宫博物院正式成立，紫禁城从皇宫转变为面向公众开放的博物馆。如今故宫博物院是世界上参观人数最多的博物馆之一。

故宫不仅是中国古代建筑艺术的巅峰之作，也是中华文明五千年历史的缩影。

After the 1911 Revolution ended imperial rule, the Palace Museum was established on October 10, 1925. The former imperial palace opened its doors to the public, and today it is one of the most visited museums in the world.

The Forbidden City is not only a masterpiece of ancient Chinese architecture but also a living testament to five thousand years of Chinese civilization.
